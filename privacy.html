Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last Updated: May 5, 2026

最后更新日期：2026年5月5日

1. Introduction / 引言

English

This Privacy Policy explains how Grid Player handles information when you use the app. We designed Grid Player primarily as a local and personal-server music player. We do not operate a cloud music library service for your music files.

中文

本隐私政策说明 Grid Player 在您使用本应用时如何处理相关信息。Grid Player 主要被设计为本地及个人服务器音乐播放器。我们不为您的音乐文件运营云端音乐资料库服务。

2. Information We Process / 我们处理的信息

English

Depending on how you use the app, Grid Player may process the following information on your device:

  • Music metadata, such as song title, artist, album, duration, year, genre, track number, cover image, lyrics, and file path.
  • Local music files or cached music files selected or downloaded by you.
  • Playlist, favorite, recent play, offline cache, and playback state data.
  • Server configuration, including server address, connection type, username, password, token, folder path, and related settings you enter.
  • Images you choose, such as custom artist portraits, playlist covers, or profile images.
  • Purchase entitlement status provided by Apple if paid subscriptions or lifetime purchases are enabled.
中文

根据您的使用方式，Grid Player 可能会在您的设备上处理以下信息：

  • 音乐元数据，例如歌曲名、艺术家、专辑、时长、年份、流派、音轨号、封面、歌词及文件路径。
  • 您选择或下载的本地音乐文件或缓存音乐文件。
  • 歌单、收藏、最近播放、离线缓存及播放状态数据。
  • 您输入的服务器配置，包括服务器地址、连接类型、用户名、密码、令牌、文件夹路径及相关设置。
  • 您选择的图片，例如自定义艺术家头像、歌单封面或个人资料图片。
  • 如果启用付费订阅或买断功能，由 Apple 提供的购买权益状态。

3. How Information Is Used / 信息用途

English

The information above is used to provide and improve app features, including:

  • Playing music and displaying your music library.
  • Connecting to your selected local, WebDAV, SMB, Emby, Jellyfin, Navidrome, Subsonic-compatible, or other supported music sources.
  • Managing playlists, favorites, albums, artists, covers, lyrics, search results, and offline cache.
  • Restoring your app settings and music library state on the same device.
  • Verifying paid feature access through Apple purchase systems when subscriptions or lifetime purchases are available.
中文

上述信息用于提供和改进应用功能，包括：

  • 播放音乐并展示您的音乐资料库。
  • 连接您选择的本地、WebDAV、SMB、Emby、Jellyfin、Navidrome、Subsonic 兼容或其他支持的音乐来源。
  • 管理歌单、收藏、专辑、艺术家、封面、歌词、搜索结果及离线缓存。
  • 在同一设备上恢复您的应用设置和音乐资料库状态。
  • 在订阅或买断功能可用时，通过 Apple 购买系统验证付费功能访问权限。

4. Local Storage and Device Processing / 本地存储与设备端处理

English

Most app data is stored and processed locally on your device, including music metadata, playlists, favorites, cached files, covers, local artist portraits, and server settings. Some information may be stored in iOS-provided storage mechanisms such as the app sandbox, local database, UserDefaults, or Keychain where applicable.

If you delete the app, reset configuration, clear cache, or remove related files, the corresponding local data may be deleted and may not be recoverable.

中文

本应用的大部分数据会在您的设备本地存储和处理，包括音乐元数据、歌单、收藏、缓存文件、封面、本地艺术家头像及服务器设置。部分信息可能会根据功能需要存储于 iOS 提供的应用沙盒、本地数据库、UserDefaults 或 Keychain 等机制中。

如果您删除应用、重置配置、清理缓存或移除相关文件，对应的本地数据可能会被删除且无法恢复。

5. Network Access and Third-Party Services / 网络访问与第三方服务

English

When you connect to a server or music source, the app communicates with the server address you provide. Your server credentials and media requests are sent to that selected server as necessary for login, scanning, metadata loading, streaming, downloading, and playback.

Grid Player does not control your third-party or self-hosted servers. Those services may collect or process information according to their own policies. Please review the privacy practices of any service you connect to.

中文

当您连接服务器或音乐来源时，本应用会与您提供的服务器地址通信。为完成登录、扫描、加载元数据、串流、下载和播放功能，您的服务器凭据及媒体请求会按需发送至您选择的服务器。

Grid Player 不控制您使用的第三方或自托管服务器。这些服务可能会依据其自身政策收集或处理信息。请您自行了解所连接服务的隐私做法。

6. Apple Payments and Subscriptions / Apple 支付与订阅

English

If paid subscriptions or lifetime purchases are added, payments will be processed by Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment card number. Apple may provide us with limited transaction or entitlement information needed to unlock paid features, restore purchases, manage subscription status, and comply with App Store requirements.

中文

如果未来加入付费订阅或终身买断，付款将由 Apple App Store 处理。我们不会接收或存储您的完整支付卡号。Apple 可能会向我们提供用于解锁付费功能、恢复购买、管理订阅状态及满足 App Store 要求所需的有限交易或权益信息。

7. Permissions / 权限说明

English

The app may request permissions only when needed for specific features, such as:

  • Local network access: to discover or connect to servers on your local network.
  • Files or media access: to import, scan, or play music files selected by you.
  • Photos access: to choose images for artist portraits, playlist covers, or profile images.
  • Network access: to connect to your configured music sources and load media content.

You can manage permissions in iOS Settings. Disabling permissions may affect related features.

中文

本应用仅会在特定功能需要时请求权限，例如：

  • 本地网络权限：用于发现或连接您本地网络中的服务器。
  • 文件或媒体访问权限：用于导入、扫描或播放您选择的音乐文件。
  • 照片访问权限：用于选择艺术家头像、歌单封面或个人资料图片。
  • 网络访问权限：用于连接您配置的音乐来源并加载媒体内容。

您可以在 iOS 设置中管理权限。关闭相关权限可能会影响对应功能。

8. Data Sharing / 信息共享

English

We do not sell your personal information. We do not upload your music files to our own server as part of the core music playback features. Information may be shared only in the following limited situations:

  • With servers or services you choose to connect, as required to provide app features.
  • With Apple, for App Store payments, subscriptions, purchase restoration, crash diagnostics, and platform services subject to Apple policies.
  • When required by applicable law, legal process, or enforceable government request.
  • With your consent or at your direction.
中文

我们不会出售您的个人信息。作为核心音乐播放功能的一部分，我们不会将您的音乐文件上传到我们自己的服务器。仅在以下有限情况下可能共享信息：

  • 为提供应用功能，与您选择连接的服务器或服务进行必要通信。
  • 与 Apple 共享，用于 App Store 支付、订阅、恢复购买、崩溃诊断及平台服务，并受 Apple 政策约束。
  • 根据适用法律、法律程序或具有执行力的政府请求而需要披露。
  • 在获得您同意或由您主动指示的情况下。
